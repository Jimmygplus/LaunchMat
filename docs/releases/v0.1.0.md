The first release of LaunchGrid: the full-screen Launchpad you remember, for macOS 26 (and macOS 14 or later).

**Install:** download `LaunchGrid-0.1.0.dmg`, open it, drag LaunchGrid into Applications, and open it. The app is signed
with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

### Features
- Full-screen grid over a blurred wallpaper, with pages, page dots, trackpad swipe, mouse wheel and keyboard navigation
- Drag to rearrange; hold at the screen edge to change pages
- Folders: drop one app onto another, rename them, drag apps out
- Search as you type, with initials (`vsc`) and pinyin for Chinese app names (`wx` → 微信)
- ⌘-click multi-select: drag several apps together, group them into a folder, or hide them
- Open with ⇧⌘Space (changeable), the Dock icon, the menu bar, a hot corner, or a trackpad pinch
- Uninstall to the Trash (hold ⌥), with a list of leftover files in `~/Library`
- Import your old Launchpad pages and folders from before macOS 26
- Launch at login; grid sizes from 7×5 to 10×6

No network access and no telemetry. GPL-3.0.
