**Pinyin search now works on an English Mac.** Typing `wx` or `weixin` finds WeChat (微信), and `bwl` finds Notes
(备忘录), whatever your system language. LaunchMat now also indexes each app's Chinese name from the app's own
localizations, not just the name shown in your current language.

**Install:** download `LaunchMat-0.1.2.dmg`, open it, drag LaunchMat into Applications, and open it. Signed with a
Developer ID and notarized by Apple. macOS 14 or later, Apple silicon and Intel. Coming from LaunchGrid 0.1.0? Your
layout and settings come along on first launch.

No network access and no telemetry. GPL-3.0.
