LaunchGrid is now **LaunchMat**. Same app, new name, and a home at [launchmat.app](https://launchmat.app).

**Install:** download `LaunchMat-0.1.1.dmg`, open it, drag LaunchMat into Applications, and open it. Signed with a
Developer ID and notarized by Apple. macOS 14 or later, Apple silicon and Intel.

**Coming from LaunchGrid 0.1.0:** on first launch LaunchMat copies your pages, folders and settings (grid size,
hotkey, hot corner, pinch) from LaunchGrid, and offers once to move the old LaunchGrid app to the Trash, so two grids
don't answer the same hotkey. LaunchGrid's own files are left untouched. If you used *Launch at Login*, turn it on
again in the LaunchMat menu.

### Changes
- Renamed to LaunchMat; bundle identifier `io.github.jimmygplus.LaunchMat`; the layout lives in
  `~/Library/Application Support/LaunchMat/layout.json`
- One-time migration of the LaunchGrid layout and settings
- Release tooling: stricter signature checks and a re-download check of every published release

No network access and no telemetry. GPL-3.0.
